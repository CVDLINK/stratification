Intended use

DomainHealthcare — Cardiology
PurposeEstimate a patient's 12-month risk of a life-threatening ventricular arrhythmia from a resting 12-lead ECG, as decision support for cardiologists.
CapabilitySupervised time-series classification on 12-lead ECG waveforms.

Out-of-scope uses

  • Pediatric patients (under 18)
  • Real-time or acute arrhythmia alarms
  • Autonomous clinical decisions without physician review
  • Patients with implanted pacemakers or defibrillators

Input / Output

InputResting 12-lead ECG (10 seconds, 500 Hz), stored as a WFDB record.
OutputA 12-month arrhythmic-risk score in [0,1] with a 95% confidence interval and a high/low band.

Cohort composition

NordECG-Arrhythmia Cohort (training)Adults referred to cardiology outpatient clinics with a suspected arrhythmia, enriched for atrial fibrillation relative to the general population. Three Nordic centres. Patients under 40 are sparsely represented and no paediatric patients are included.

Caveats & limitations

  • Trained mainly on a Northern-European population; generalisability elsewhere not yet validated.
  • Performance degrades on noisy ECGs (lead detachment, motion artefact).
  • Not validated for patients with implanted cardiac devices.
  • 12-month prediction horizon; not suitable for acute risk alerting.

Groups with lower performance

  • Patients under 40 (under-represented in training data).
  • Patients with atrial fibrillation (differing signal morphology).
